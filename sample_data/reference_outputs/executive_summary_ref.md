# Executive Summary: National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2024

## Strategic Overview
India's 2024 National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence (NSAI) positions the country to become a global AI leader through the ₹10,300 crore AI Mission (2024-2029). The strategy builds on the 2018 foundation, incorporating lessons learned and the imperative for sovereign AI capabilities across seven strategic pillars.

## Key Findings & Evidence

- **Budget Allocation**: ₹10,300 crore over five years, with 44.3% (₹4,564 cr) for compute infrastructure, 19.1% (₹1,971 cr) for foundational models, and 8.0% (₹826 cr) for curated datasets.

- **Compute Target**: 10,000+ GPU sovereign cluster delivering 100+ PFLOPs by 2029 (baseline: 5 PFLOPs in 2024), with 50% renewable energy mandate by 2027.

- **Foundational Models**: Development of 5+ Indic-language LLMs (22 languages, 100B+ parameters) and domain-specific models for healthcare, agriculture, legal, and finance sectors.

- **Healthcare Impact**: Target deployment of AI diagnostics in 50,000 Health & Wellness Centres by 2029, addressing the 1:834 doctor-patient ratio (WHO norm: 1:1000).

- **Agriculture Reach**: AI advisories to reach 10 crore farmers by 2027 (baseline: 2 crore), covering precision farming, supply chain optimization, and climate resilience.

- **Education Target**: Train 10 lakh AI professionals by 2029 (current: 4.16 lakh), with mandatory AI literacy from Grade 6 and personalized learning in 22 languages.

- **Smart Cities**: 100 cities with AI-enabled command centres for traffic, waste, safety, and service delivery by 2029.

- **Governance Framework**: Risk-based AI classification aligned with DPDP Act 2023, algorithmic transparency for high-risk AI, bias audit mandates, and regulatory sandbox for innovation.

- **International Leadership**: Active participation in GPAI, bilateral partnerships with US/EU/Japan/Israel/UAE, and standardization in ISO/IEC JTC1 and BIS.

## Statistics & Implications
- **Economic Impact**: Global AI market projected at $1.8T by 2030 (37.3% CAGR); Generative AI could add $2.6-4.4T annually.
- **Talent Base**: India ranks 1st in AI Skill Penetration (Stanford AI Index 2023) with 16% of global AI talent.
- **Investment Structure**: 60% Central, 20% State, 15% Industry, 5% International/Other funding.
- **Monitoring**: Quarterly public dashboards, annual independent evaluation, mid-term review in 2027.

## Supported Actions
1. Accelerate sovereign compute procurement with green energy mandates
2. Launch Indic LLM development program with open-source release strategy
3. Establish National Data Platform with 500+ curated datasets by 2029
3. Deploy mission-mode AI applications in 5 priority sectors
4. Integrate AI curriculum from Grade 6 through PhD levels
5. Operationalize risk-based regulatory framework with sectoral alignment
