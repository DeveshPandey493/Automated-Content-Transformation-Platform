# Social Media Posts: India's National AI Strategy 2024

## LinkedIn Post (Professional Format)

---

🚀 **India's AI Moment Has Arrived**

The Cabinet has approved the **₹10,300 crore AI Mission** (2024-2029) — India's most ambitious technology initiative since the space program.

As someone working at the intersection of technology and public policy, three things stand out:

**1. Sovereign Compute at Population Scale** 🇮🇳
10,000+ GPUs delivering 100+ PFLOPs. Not borrowed cloud credits — our own infrastructure. 50% renewable energy by 2027. This changes what's possible for Indian researchers and startups.

**2. AI That Speaks Your Language** 🗣️
5+ foundational models across 22 Indian languages. Not fine-tunes — ground-up Indic LLMs. Healthcare diagnostics in Odia. Agricultural advisories in Marathi. Legal aid in Tamil. This is inclusion by design.

**3. ₹10,300 Crore With Accountability** 📊
- 44% compute infrastructure
- 19% foundational models  
- 8% curated datasets
- 12% mission-mode applications
- 7% skilling (10 lakh professionals by 2029)
- Risk-based governance aligned with DPDP Act 2023

**The Numbers That Matter:**
• 50,000 health centres with AI diagnostics by 2029
• 10 crore farmers reached with AI advisories by 2027
• 10 lakh AI professionals trained by 2029
• 100 smart cities with AI command centres

**Why This Matters:**
India ranks #1 globally in AI skill penetration (Stanford AI Index 2023). We have the talent. Now we're building the infrastructure to keep it here.

The strategy isn't a PDF — it's a ₹10,300 crore commitment with quarterly public dashboards, annual independent audits, and a mid-term review in 2027.

**For the ecosystem:** Compute credits for startups. Open-source Indic models. Regulatory sandbox. Public procurement preference for Made-in-India AI.

**For citizens:** Your data protected by DPDP Act. Right to explanation for AI decisions. Grievance redressal for AI harms. Inclusive design mandatory.

This is how a democratic nation builds AI — transparently, inclusively, and at population scale.
