# Public Advisory: India's National AI Strategy 2024 - What It Means for You

## Title
**India's AI Mission: Empowering Citizens Through Artificial Intelligence**
*Issued by NITI Aayog, Government of India | June 2024*

## Audience
All Citizens of India | Students | Farmers | Healthcare Seekers | Small Business Owners

## Situation
The Government of India has launched the **AI Mission** with an investment of **₹10,300 crore** (2024-2029) to develop Artificial Intelligence that serves every Indian. This advisory explains how AI will impact your daily life and what protections are in place.

## Verified Facts & Advisories

### 🏥 For Healthcare Access
- **AI Diagnostics Coming to Your Local Clinic**: By 2029, 50,000 Health & Wellness Centres will have AI tools for TB screening, cancer detection, and eye exams
- **Language No Barrier**: AI health assistants will work in 22 Indian languages
- **Your Data Protected**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ensures your health data stays private and secure

### 🌾 For Farmers
- **Free AI Advisories**: 10 crore farmers will receive crop, soil, and pest advice by 2027 via mobile apps
- **Fair Prices**: AI-powered price forecasting helps you decide when and where to sell
- **Climate Alerts**: Early drought and flood warnings to protect your crops

### 🎓 For Students & Parents
- **AI in Schools from Grade 6**: Mandatory AI literacy curriculum in all schools
- **Learning in Your Language**: Personalized educational content in 22 scheduled languages
- **10 Lakh AI Jobs by 2029**: New career opportunities in AI development, data science, and applications

### 🏙️ For Urban Residents
- **Smarter Cities**: 100 cities getting AI traffic management, waste optimization, and faster emergency response
- **Easier Government Services**: Chatbots and automated systems for certificates, complaints, and benefits
- **Cleaner Environment**: AI-optimized waste collection and recycling

### 💼 For Small Businesses
- **AI Tools Access**: Subsidized compute credits for startups and MSMEs
- **Government Procurement**: Preference for Made-in-India AI solutions in public tenders
- **Skilling Programs**: Free/low-cost AI training through industry partnerships

## Precautions for Citizens

### 🔒 Your Digital Rights
1. **Consent Required**: No AI system can use your personal data without explicit consent (DPDP Act 2023)
2. **Right to Explanation**: If an AI makes a decision affecting you (loan, benefit, job), you can demand an explanation
3. **Opt-Out Available**: You can choose non-AI alternatives for government services
4. **Complaint Mechanism**: Dedicated grievance portal for AI-related issues

### ⚠️ Be Aware Of
- **Deepfakes & Scams**: Verify video/audio calls claiming to be from officials
- **Data Sharing**: Read privacy policies before using AI apps
- **Job Transitions**: Some roles will change; reskilling programs available free of cost

## Key Government Commitments

✅ **No Surveillance State**: AI not for mass monitoring of citizens
✅ **No Job Loss Without Support**: Reskilling before automation displacement
✅ **No Bias in Public AI**: Mandatory bias audits for all government AI systems
✅ **No Data Misuse**: Strict penalties under DPDP Act 2023 for violations
✅ **Inclusive Design**: AI must work for persons with disabilities, elderly, and remote communities
